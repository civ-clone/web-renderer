# Free 3D art for the 3D map: a licence survey

Slice 2.0 of [the 3D map's plan](3d-renderer-plan.md), for the units and Wonders that D3 of
[the spec](3d-renderer.md) takes from CC0 and CC BY models, and the textures and plants a hosted set can bring.

Checked 2026-11-18. Research only: no model or texture file was downloaded, and no account used. 86 rows in all, the
best candidates first in each table.

- **What passes:** CC0, CC BY or public domain, stated by the author on the asset's own page or in its download, and
  realistic rather than stylised (D2). ShareAlike, NonCommercial, NoDerivatives, "royalty free", "free for mods",
  Mixamo's terms and game rips all fail, as does anything whose licence isn't stated where it can be read.
- **Before a model is used** (slices 2.2, 5.2 and 6.4), its licence is read again on its page, and its files checked
  for what the page doesn't show (an embedded skeleton that's someone else's, say). A licence on the page isn't proof
  the work is the uploader's own: rows whose notes say provenance isn't stated are checked further first. A pack becomes a hosted set only
  once it has passed, and every CC BY model is credited by name in the game's art credits list (D3).
- **Spot-checked** against Sketchfab's own records when this was added: the Spartan, the Cleveland Museum's armour
  and the 1815 infantry, each with the licence line, author and face count given here.

## How I checked

- **Sketchfab.** I shortlisted models through Sketchfab's public API (`/v3/search`, with `downloadable=true` and `license=cc0` or `license=by`). For every row I then read the model record (`/v3/models/{uid}`), which carries the same licence line the model page shows:
  - **"CC Attribution"**: "Author must be credited. Commercial use is allowed.", linking to http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  - **"CC0 Public Domain"**: "Credit is not mandatory. Commercial use is allowed."

  I read every description for game rips, Mixamo, AI generation and other people's work, and quote any extra statement the author makes in the Notes column.
- **Look.** I graded look from Sketchfab's own thumbnails, viewed in a browser. "Not seen" means I didn't see the model, and "fair" covers anything in between.
- **Polygons and format.** Polygon counts are Sketchfab's face counts. Texture figures are the count and largest size in Sketchfab's auto-converted GLB. Every downloadable Sketchfab model offers glTF/GLB, and the uploader's original files (FBX, BLEND and so on) come in the source zip.
- **Attribution.** For every CC BY row, use Sketchfab's own form of credit: `"Title" (model URL) by Author (profile URL), licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)`. CC BY 4.0 also asks you to say the work was changed, for example "decimated, textures converted to KTX2". A credits page in the game and a CREDITS file next to the hosted assets covers both.
- **Practical note.** Most scans here are 0.5 to 4.5 million faces with 8K textures. They need decimating or retopologising, baking down and transcoding to KTX2 before they suit a strategy map.

The strongest sources:
- **Andy Woodhead** on Sketchfab: historical soldiers, a chariot, a cannon and a WWI tank, all CC BY and "free to use in any way".
- **Animal Animator**: rigged and animated elephant and horse, CC BY.
- **Heritage bodies**: the Finnish Heritage Agency and the Swedish National Maritime and Transport Museums (ships, CC BY). Smithsonian, the Cleveland Museum of Art and Virtual Museums of Małopolska (CC0 museum scans).
- **Poly Haven and ambientCG**: CC0 ground textures and plants.
- **Quaternius' Universal Animation Library 1 and 2**: CC0 motion data.

**The practical unit pipeline** has three parts:
1. Take Woodhead's UE4-mannequin-rigged soldiers, or a MakeHuman body for civilians.
2. Retarget CC0 Universal Animation Library clips onto them, and CC BY 100STYLE mocap for walk, run and idle.
   - UAL's pages name 8-direction locomotion, jog, sprint, death, "combat and gun", a sword swing (in a changelog) and melee combos (UAL 2).
   - They don't name idle or walk, so confirm the free tiers have them before relying on UAL alone.
3. Export glTF with the clips embedded.

Woodhead's rigs target Epic's UE4 mannequin skeleton ("selecting the UE4_Mannequin for the skeleton"). I couldn't check without downloading whether his FBX files embed Epic's skeleton asset or just match its bone names. Check that before shipping, or re-rig onto the MakeHuman or UAL skeleton.

Animals come rigged and animated already.

- **Civ4 units** are typically new meshes put into Firaxis NIF skeletons and KFM animations. ambrox62 reportedly credits "models and textures released by firaxis and other forum modders". These are derived from Firaxis's files, so they fail.

- **Moar Units** (Deliverator) reportedly mixes Firaxis animation sets, Civ5 conversions and Europa Barbarorum assets, so it fails as a pack.
- **sman1975** reportedly gives blanket permission to use anything in his mods, but that covers his own Civ5 mods, not other modellers' art.

That is a clear written permission to redistribute, but not CC0, CC BY or a public-domain dedication, so it fails the strict bar. It's worth deciding on because it could fill attack and death clips. I did not check which fighting takes it holds.
- **mgg8's CC0 aircraft** on Sketchfab (about 100, from the P-51D to the F-35B and a V-1). The licence is clear, but several say they were "Created in Blender with AI assistance", which the bar treats as needing a decision, as other AI-made uploads are unverified below. The Spitfire in table 5 says only "Created procedurally in Blender".
- **[NASA 3D Resources](https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources)** (Saturn V and others).
  - The README says the assets are "free and without copyright". The usage guidelines say NASA content "generally are not subject to copyright in the United States" and ask that "NASA should be acknowledged as the source of the material".
  - Status outside the US isn't stated, and "The NASA Insignia, Logotype ... are not in the public domain".
  - Contributors include non-NASA firms, so check each model's origin.

## Fails on licence or provenance (near misses)

- **Giza Pyramid Complex (from Civilization VI)**, ChrisCLP: "The Gyza Pyramid Complex from the video game Civ VI". A recreation of Firaxis's design.
- **Colossus**, Reclaimer017 (and an identical copy by Pofdpoi): the description calls it the first boss "at the beginning of God of War II", a game rip.
- **Hoover dam** and **Vatican and St. Peter Basilica**, gnaz: "Imported from Google Maps with RenderDoc and Blender."
- **Temple of Apollo - Naxos Greece (CyArk Dataset)**, vashar: labelled CC BY, but CyArk's Data Use Policy licenses its data "under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International License".
- **German Bundeswehr soldier** and **German KSK operator**, 42manako: "Model ripped from Call to Arms, rigged with Mixamo."
- **CAESAR SPG**, also 42manako: "Not my original model."
- **Paratrooper**, SidewalkNZ: a Toy Story 3 rip.
- **M109A1 Paladin SPH**, Jeyhun1985: the same face count (168,906) as a "M109A1 (War Thunder)" upload, so probably a rip.
- **planeta-elefante's elephants**: several titled "(Zoo Tycoon 2)", so treat the whole account as rips.
- **Parameswara Chronicles soldiers and knights**, helmi.norman: "rigged in Mixamo" (Mixamo's terms).
- **Medieval Knight | Sculpture | Game ready**, by__Rx: the mesh is CC BY and usable, but "I use Mixamo for the idle animation", so drop the clip.
- **0 A.D. (Wildfire Games) art**: realistic historical units, but CC BY-SA, as reported by the [Creative Commons case study](https://wiki.creativecommons.org/wiki/Case_Studies/0_A.D.) and OpenGameArt's copies. ShareAlike fails the bar.
- **BlenderKit "Royalty Free" assets**: its [licensing FAQ](https://www.blenderkit.com/docs/licenses/licensing-faq/) says games may use them "but the assets shouldn't be directly extractable in the distribution", which a public web game can't promise. Its [CC0 position](https://www.blenderkit.com/articles/our-position-on-cc0-models/) asks people "not to upload CC0 models" made by others, so CC0 items there are few.
- **Game and franchise fan art labelled CC BY on Sketchfab**: Fortnite, Halo, Star Wars, Hollow Knight, Lethal Company, Black Myth: Wukong and the like. Excluded throughout.

## Unverified (not presented as passing)

- **AI-generated uploads labelled CC BY**: 3dUVpro ("generated using the free Mesha AI Toolkit"), kmkrtchyan70's legionaries and longbowman, jungle_jim's Rodin models. I didn't check the generators' terms, and the look is often lumpy.

The site has more CC0 objects, flagged per item under "Media Usage"; si.edu refused automated fetches.

- **itch.io**: nothing realistic, CC0 or CC BY and rigged turned up beyond the Quaternius animation libraries.
